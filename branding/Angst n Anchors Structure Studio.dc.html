Drafting panels in paper, the build floats on dark water. The voxel layer system is promoted to the toolbar: deck stepper, ISOLATE (hide other decks) and GHOST (deck above as dashed outline — visible in the viewport). Selection is brass everywhere at once: toolbox tile, viewport cell + callout, explorer row, properties. The bottom strip earns its place with build-consequence data — weight, draft, stability — so builders feel the boat, not just the blocks. FLOAT TEST is the one loud button. At 1080p: chips 16 px, tree rows 20 px, status 17 px.
