Coastal terrain pass - generation 9

Preserves original mainland and branching fjord graph. Adds shelf-attached island
clusters and anisotropic coastal rock/fracture deformation. The final signed
shore field drives exposed land, seabed shelves, charts, ports and seamarks.
A two-pass distance transform restores metre-scale distances after deformation;
otherwise noise slopes compress shelves and invalidate fixed placement offsets.
Central fjord corridors remain open; existing minimum-width tests are retained.
Standard geography is now 513 samples (78.125 m) instead of 257 (156.25 m).
Tiny metre-scale isolated rocks are still below this resolution.

Removed the independent bank-noise shallow patches. Sea-level datum now agrees
with the signed shoreline: land samples are physically above mean water level.
Low shoreline/skerry surfaces reuse the rock material across near/far terrain.
Chart overview increased to 512 pixels. Zoom tiles remain cached.

Pole spacing and port-anchor exclusion reduced from 700/1000 m to 450/450 m.
Placement searches the physical shelf; direction still validates a deeper sector.
Showcase now uses the production streaming layer, not a manually placed prop.
It captures the streamed pole, boat approach, aerial terrain and production chart.

Checks: world_layout_generator_test, seabed_test, coastal_seamarks_test,
coastal_terrain_review across seeds 42, 1212599375, 77127. Each has 35 ports;
204, 216 and 246 poles respectively. Determinism, fjord continuity, water/land
agreement, mesh edges, depth/chart agreement, pole yaw and streaming checked.
Generation test measured about 11 seconds and about 1 microsecond per query.
These are generation/query timings, not an FPS benchmark.
