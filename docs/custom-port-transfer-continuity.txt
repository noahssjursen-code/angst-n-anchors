Custom port transfer continuity — 15 October 2026

Opening an office door previously stopped every active crane/grab/pump because
the transfer facade reset on every newer snapshot. The suspended container or
interpolated fish/bulk fill then jumped directly to its committed destination.
The focused reproduction failed for all three cargo families.

CustomPortTransferPresenter now keeps playback for a deliberately narrow set of
contiguous, receipt-matched authority updates:
- edit_assets changing only door_open or wall_colour on existing, unrelated
  decoration placements;
- rename_port changing only the harbour name.

All other document fields, stores, vessel records, reservations, derived berths,
and existing receipts must remain identical. The selected equipment, store,
ship, installed cargo interfaces and container destinations must remain the same
live nodes at the same stationary world transforms. A door's full rotation
envelope must stay clear of the active handling bounds. The presenter applies
the new door/name immediately without rebuilding the unchanged handling nodes.

This is visual continuity, not another job authority. Committed cargo records
remain authoritative. New transfers, revision gaps, unaccompanied snapshots,
departures, cancellations, replaced resources, changed poses and unknown edits
continue to reset playback and reveal the accepted destination state. A nearby
door that intersects the conservative handling bounds may still interrupt;
this deliberately favors safe cleanup over assuming new geometry is harmless.

The replacement checks also exposed existing freed-object cleanup failures:
assigning a freed crane/pump to a typed variable raised before the validity
check. Container, bulk and catch cleanup now validate such references before
typed access, so replaced equipment does not strand jobs or temporary visuals.
