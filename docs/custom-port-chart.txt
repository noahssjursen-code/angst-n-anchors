CUSTOM PORTS ON THE MARINE CHART

CustomPortChartProjection.project(snapshot, catalog, derived_berths = []) produces
a passive JSON-safe public summary from an already validated construction record.
It includes identity, display name, company reference, world identity, world-space
origin, exact platform polygons, exposed edges and the constructed berth count.
That count describes construction, not current booking availability or services.
Store contents, vessels, reservations, accounts and command receipts are excluded.

CustomPortChartProjection.chart_snapshot(layout, summaries) adapts those summaries
to an isolated ChartDataSnapshot. It never writes PortCatalog or registers NPCs,
trade routes, production services, home-port choices or teleport destinations.

The existing ChartHarbourPlan explicitly handles source=custom before resolving
any generated-port data. It cannot call PortExpander for these records. Each real
foundation footprint is filled separately, preserving triangles, chamfers, yaw,
concave outlines and inner holes. Only exposed edges are drawn, so a tiled floor
does not become an unreadable grid. Clicking built deck selects its port; empty
holes do not acquire a solid bounding-box hit area. The existing origin marker
still has its ordinary small click target.

Selection shows the port name, constructed berth count, Company ref and range.
Unknown company IDs are not presented as resolved company names. No generated
imports/exports, population, ship class or live berth occupancy are inferred.
Custom records are explicitly blocked from the existing home-port confirmation.

WORKSHOP / TRAVEL

The workshop refreshes its chart from the current construction record. In the
travel review, M or the Chart button opens the current document collection and
focuses the selected port. Escape/Close returns to the prior fly/cursor state.
Charts read the latest retained record even when that port's models are unloaded.
Imported saved documents remain read-only. This is presentation only; actual MP
directory/event integration is deliberately not added by the chart adapter.
