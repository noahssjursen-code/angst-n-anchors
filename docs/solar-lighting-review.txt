SOLAR MOTION AND COASTAL SHADOWS - 7 October 2026

Root causes:
WeatherLighting compared time-of-day to the preceding frame before overwriting
it. At normal frame rates each step was below the notification threshold, so
sun lighting waited for unrelated weather events while the sky disk moved.
Terrain LOD >= 1 explicitly disabled shadow casting. The sun also covered only
180 metres, excluding the landscape from coastal shadow reception.

Changes:
Accumulate time against the last published value (including midnight wrapping).
Update celestial light directions and ocean solar direction each frame.
Retain throttled colour/environment updates, without forcing FFT weather work
for every clock frame. Restore shadow casting on all streamed terrain LODs.
Four cascades cover 6 km, with splits at 90/360/1500 metres and blended borders.
Water shading, wave simulation, world geography and saves are unchanged.

Checks:
solar_coast_review: rendered A/B with a 400 m high occluder 1.5 km away.
Bright direct reflection disappears in its shadow; sky illumination remains.
240 small clock steps followed by the actual light; 48 lighting notifications.
world_lighting_grade_test: passed all presentation checks.
live_lighting_review: full seeded world, public spawn and owned vessel deck at
night/dawn/noon, plus sun/moon skies. No logged engine errors. Boot ~10 seconds.
1280x720 RTX 5070 local performance sample, vsync off: 180m two-cascade mean
8.86ms / 2041 draws; 6km four-cascade mean 7.43ms / 3043 draws. Sequential
samples contain warm-up/streaming noise; this is NOT a speedup claim. Long range
adds shadow draws and needs profiling on lower-end hardware.

Limits:
Shadow reception is finite at 6 km; this is not planetary horizon shadowing.
Only loaded terrain geometry can cast. A separate existing streaming issue
appears as distant forest without terrain in the early live capture; it is not
certified by these lighting tests.
Terrain material audit: TerrainSurfaceMaps still synthesizes 256px noise maps;
terrain.gdshader tints those channels instead of using sourced ground materials.
The farthest LOD uses vertex colours plus canopy. Real rock/soil/heath textures,
metre scale and near/far material consistency remain a distinct material pass.
