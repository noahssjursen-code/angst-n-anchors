LAYOUT AND ASSETS
The shared data fixture contains 1,019 placements and four derived berths:
two container-quay slots, a projecting bulk berth, and a sheltered fishing berth.
A western pier and inlet, deeper bulk apron, 45-degree heads and varied wharf
depths replace the rectangular outline. A broad shore approach connects the site.
Crane dimensions and existing cargo services determine the working apron size.
The container yard remains unmarked asphalt, without the rejected yellow grid.

Five timber buildings use player-placeable parts: red net loft and warehouse,
ochre workshop, blue chandlery and a taller ivory harbour office. Gabled charcoal
roofs, signs, gutters, downpipes, covered porch, rope coils, net cages, benches,
guardrails, shore cabinets and bulk retaining walls add detail at walking scale.
The library now renders 54 actual placement thumbnails. The Blender manifest
contains 45 authored/reused assets; other library entries are existing factories.

Buildings are EXTERIOR DECORATIONS. Doors and roller shutters are closed and
solid. Windows are opaque reflective panes. The upper office is a facade only:
no staircase, interior floor or accessible room. NPCs and services remain outside.
Existing entrance asset IDs are retained so old port documents still load.
Older door_open attributes do not open the decorative assets.

Angled foundations include 4 m and 8 m pieces. They share square-pad concrete,
extend below the water to the seabed, and use exact polygon collision. Mitred
coping follows the diagonal boundaries without overlapping top faces.
Every placement remains on the common sea-referenced deck datum.
