The harbour reads as three repeated fingers attached to a large black apron.
Road strips stop without a connected inland route. Buildings and goods areas
do not establish clear loading entrances, circulation or a waterfront street.
Small repeated houses float visually apart from the industrial working area.
Cyan/orange building blocks are temporary placeholders for archived legacy
blueprints; they are not the intended finished building models.

Asphalt is unshaded flat colour, unlike the lit quay surfaces. Raised road
meshes are visual geometry without matching separate collision. There are few
player-scale cues: coping, fenders, ladders, drains, markings and service points.
The showcase's large diagnostic panels also obscure composition.

Physics finding and repair
--------------------------
Visible foundation triangles previously used averaged rectangular collision
slabs. At bends these left holes and could extend beyond the visible boundary.
The real showcase had 75 unsupported points among 2,703 sampled asphalt points.
A production player capsule dropped 2.79 m through one such location. Terrain
collision is intentionally disabled in this showcase; this does not establish
that players fall through the entire world in normal gameplay.

Foundation collision now uses shallow convex prisms from the actual top-face
triangles. The same showcase has zero missing support samples and zero sampled
foundation support outside the visible outline. Shape count rose from 25 to
100; broad performance testing across the largest ports remains necessary.
This repairs the floor, not the full submerged quay-wall collision.

Regression: tests/port_surface_collision_test.tscn, with --shipyard-playtest.
The bent fixture previously missed 172 of 2,568 surface samples and produced
25 outside-floor samples. Both counts are now zero. It also settles the actual
production player at bends and the first previously unsupported fixture point.
Walking across all seams, pier transitions and terrain remains a follow-up.

Player capsule is 1.8 m high, radius 0.35 m. Visual clothing/body changes do not
automatically change that envelope. Houses and several land trade props are
visual-only in their construction code. Audit imported equipment individually
before calling it solid. Keep interaction query shapes separate from physical
obstacles; avoid tiny hoses and trim snagging the capsule.

Design direction
----------------
Keep seeded coastlines, berth identities, authority commands and persisted
port graphs. Rework the layout recipe and presentation around connected use:

1. Plan navigable water, berth frontage, mooring leads and crane reach first.
   Select continuous quay, basin, L-shaped quay or finger pier to suit the site.
   Do not express every commodity as another copy of the same long pier.
2. Allocate working yards behind the frontage: fish landing to sorting/cold
   storage; general cargo to laydown/warehouse; bulk to handling/storage;
   service berth to fuel, water, waste and repair access.
3. Connect yards and building loading doors to a land entrance. Reserve vehicle
   circulation, turning space and pedestrian access before placing decoration.
4. Generate paving and walk collision from the same closed surface geometry.
   Explicitly model grade changes, retaining edges and quay walls. Use markings
   for painted lanes, not raised black blocks pretending to be roads.
5. Place buildings facing their access routes. Give them loading doors, canopies,
   foundations and appropriate service yards instead of scattered boxes.

Primary reference: https://sortland-havn.no/kaier-og-kapasitet/
The harbour authority describes distinct terminal, fishing, storage and service
functions, including cold/dry storage and shore services. The connected-yard
layout above is our game design interpretation, not a copied surveyed plan.

Reusable Blender kit, in construction order
-------------------------------------------
A. Quay coping straight/corner/end pieces, seawall panels, rubber fenders and
   brackets, rescue ladder, safety rail with deliberate openings at moorings.
B. Curbs, ramps, drain channels/grates, manholes, shore-power/water cabinets,
   light poles, hydrant/rescue stations and waste enclosures.
C. Modular industrial building walls, corners, roofs, loading doors, personnel
   doors, windows, canopies and dock platforms. Fish sheds and larger warehouses
   should share construction rules without becoming identical silhouettes.
D. Contextual yard objects: fish tubs, pallets, cages and handling equipment.
   Reuse existing container, bollard and landing equipment assets where sound.

Each model needs metric dimensions, predictable placement origin, exposed
material regions where useful, documented sockets and intentional collision.
Use authored Blender geometry with bevels, supports and proper joins. Keep
small cosmetic detail out of walking collision. Doors/lights retain independent
parts for interaction; decoration must not claim unavailable gameplay.
Use lit rough asphalt with stable world scale, subtle aggregate and restrained
repairs; concrete gets edge wear and a tidal band where appropriate. Avoid
uniform noise covering every surface. Review from sea and actual eye height.
