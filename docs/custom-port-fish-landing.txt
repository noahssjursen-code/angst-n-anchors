Custom port fish landing
========================

The construction library now includes Fish landing plant. It reuses the four
Blender-authored port_kit landing modules, the existing refrigerated seawater
tank bank and the existing flexible-hose animation. This is an isolated custom
port cargo workflow; it does not sell fish, pay a company, register production
port services or call WorldGateway.

Placement and physical dimensions
---------------------------------
The complete bundle is 20 x 16 metres and at most 5.35 metres high. The player
places it on the normal common deck plane, with a 4 m equipment grid and
90-degree rotations. Its full footprint is validated, but its centre is not
restricted to 20 x 16 m tiles: it fits a 32 m pier. Terrain/cursor height does
not change this plane. Foundation tiles still snap by their own full size.
It requires supporting foundations; it never supplies invisible extra ground.

Local -Z is toward the water. The tank bank sits at (-3, 0, 0), at original
scale. The landing skid sits at (6, 0, -1), yaw 90 degrees. Its authored suction
socket is at (5.18, 1.35, -4.72) in bundle coordinates. The service radius is
24 metres from this socket. Berth rules conservatively check the complete hull
in plan; presentation also checks the actual installed tank's 3D hose endpoint.

The bank occupies X [-8.2, 2.35], Z [-5.75, 5.75]. The skid occupies
X [4.1, 7.9], Z [-4.725, 3.63]. A 1.75-metre aisle separates them. Its transfer
pipe crosses overhead. Seven equipment-shaped colliders protect machinery,
tanks and the low bank plinth without blocking the whole plot. Static mesh
bounds are checked against the published footprint. Metal, paint and concrete
reuse SurfaceMaterialLibrary with a metre-scale rest frame; sight glasses keep
their optical appearance. No painted yard markings are added.

Finite storage and service identity
-----------------------------------
Placing a new plant creates exactly one EMPTY 40,000 kg catch store. It accepts
fresh_groundfish, with catch handling capability. Visitor loading/unloading
start disabled; existing owner access/settings govern these permissions.
The attached_store_only flag prevents the fixed pipe from serving some other
bank through a fabricated store_ids link. Container/bulk cranes still retain
their existing shared-yard arrangement.

Species, quality, catch time/position and individual specimen dimensions remain
in saved lot attributes. The tank bank reconstructs its normal CatchLot read
model from those lots. Extra saved attributes remain in the authoritative port
record even if the visual tank does not use them. The bank never writes back to
that record. No fish are generated by the visual or by constructing the plant.

Transfer presentation API
-------------------------
CustomPortTransferPresenter is the existing facade for container, bulk and
catch playback. Its catch presenter validates adjacent committed revisions,
the issued receipt, selected pump/store/vessel, exact lot quantity/mass delta
and the allocation produced by CustomPortVesselProjection.catch_hold_plan.
Only catch lots enter that plan. Canonical installed catch identities now match
ImportedDraftVessel (tank_<placement index>).

FishLandingPump.start_projection(ship, hold, mass_kg, loading, reach_m) and
step_projection(delta) form the explicit visual-only path. It animates hose
connection, pumping and flushing, and emits projection_completed. It does not
call start_unload, withdraw/accept inventory, production transfer_completed,
FishingLandingService or WorldGateway. Existing production start_unload retains
its previous behavior. Direction text distinguishes pumping ashore and aboard.

ShoreRswTankBank.apply_projection replaces the read model without emitting a
production inventory event. show_transfer_mass changes only sight-glass fill;
snapshot/inventory always report the already committed mass. Completion or
interruption clears that visual override. A bank refuses overflowing projection
instead of silently truncating it.

Duplicate receipts do not restart a hose. A missed revision reconstructs the
latest snapshot without replaying history. Departure, rebuilding, a changed
connection or explicit reset retracts the hose and restores committed contents.
The scene does not become cargo authority because an animation is running.

Reproducible verification
------------------------
Run tests/custom_port_fish_landing_test.tscn with --shipyard-playtest and an
isolated APPDATA directory. Add --capture in a native rendered run. The fixture
constructs the bundle through the real editor, docks the actual fishing_hook_line
vessel with 300 kg cod and one 18.25 kg halibut, unloads/reloads both kinds,
interrupts a connected hose by departing, and simulates a missed revision.

The rendered test includes the real Player crossing 13.5 metres of working
aisle without jumping. Headless DisplayServer cannot capture the mouse, so it
does not claim to exercise this native movement check. Both modes check physical
equipment collision and the exact actual hold identity/connection.

15 October 2026: focused native test passed 66 checks with a clean runtime log.
Archived native pictures include the close aisle/material view, empty plant,
connected unloading/loading hoses and final committed state. All are saved in
C:/Users/noahs/Pictures/machinescreenshots and visually inspected.

The existing production catch_hold_test passed its capacity, FIFO, serialization,
vessel mass and stop/restart/full-bank landing checks. Its immediate headless
shutdown still reports 15 audio-only resources (AudioStreamWAV/PlaybackWAV);
verbose inspection showed no leaked scene nodes. The custom-port test drains
its audio buffer and exits cleanly. Shared bulk/grain transfer regression also
passed 28 checks after adding the catch path.

This does not yet install a production fish market, real-time trade pricing,
live MP authority or fishing contract settlement. The existing operator opens
the workshop's cargo controls; no production FishLandingEquipmentJob is created.
