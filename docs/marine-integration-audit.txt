1. The first propulsion integration needs actual hull-specific mounts

Actual Godot inspection scene:
  scenes/showcases/marine_asset_audit_showcase.tscn
Keys 1/2 show the stern exterior or hide the hull to inspect the old hardware.
The propeller rotates about Godot Z and rudder about Y. This is an explicitly
labelled inspection animation, NOT connected gameplay integration. The scene is
recognized by ShipyardPlaytestMode before autoload startup, including F6.

ImportedDraftVessel.configure() uses CatalogHullVessel.make_physics_profile():
propeller force point (0, .584, 6.3), rudder force point (0, .7008, 6.44).
Those are force application coordinates, not vetted visual sockets. At these
coordinates the existing bronze_propeller and rudder GLBs are hidden inside
the stern in the actual render. Do not simply instantiate them there and call
propellers implemented.

Measured neutral imported bounds at these mounts:
- Propeller: x +/- .377632; y .206368..961633; z 6.17..6.43.
- Rudder: x +/- .04; y .7008..2.0508; z 6.23..6.65.
The propeller's longitudinal span overlaps the rudder's. The old rudder model
origin is its bottom, not a stock mounting datum. Their surrounding hull is
solid: the hull generator closes the transom at z=7, with centre-bottom y=.28.
Thus these earlier marine-kit experiments are not approved direct replacements.

Next package decision: author separate shaft/stern-tube support, propeller and
rudder source/export assets with named shaft and stock pivots and a documented
mount manifest. Fit their swept envelopes outside the actual underwater shell,
with rudder aft of propeller and no full-lock intersections. Preserve the exact
deck lattice and outline. If an underwater aperture is needed, author it in
Blender, verify resulting collision and do not silently move physics force points
to solve visual placement. Do not rescale a whole boat to make hardware fit.

PropulsionComponent throttle is negative ahead, positive astern. Its dry-fuel
early return currently leaves delivered_thrust_n stale from the preceding tick.
Fix and test that before using the value to drive presentation. RudderComponent
uses the requested throttle to estimate prop-wash even when fuel is empty;
record this as a separate existing physical issue, rather than changing handling
inside an art adapter. Local presentation can read component state; remote
presentation needs confirmed snapshots (do not invent a second control loop).
Choose a documented visual RPM curve: there is no measured shaft RPM state yet.

2. Hull and construction pipeline boundaries

The accepted hull is TrawlerHullAsset: 14 x 5 m, deck 2.92 m, half-metre
construction corners, 2:1 and 1:1 bow edges. ImportedShipPartsEditor assembles
the authored kit; ImportedDraftVessel reuses it with normal BoatBody components.
Imported runtime currently hardcodes the 14 m profile and 58 t / 300 kW.

The old hull catalog contains 70x18 through 150x32 entries and explicitly says
its dimensions are twice real scale. HullCatalog._normalize does not simply
halve these dimensions. ContainerUnit.DEFAULT_SIZE_M is 4.0. This conflicts with
the accepted metre-based Blender/player kit. Do not copy old catalog sizes or
power blindly into new ships. Measure cargo footprints in the actual game,
choose new hull dimensions explicitly, and preserve old IDs/save data.

VesselSpawn.instantiate_from_record still follows registered legacy brick
records and DeckFitout. Builder drafts have NOT become production fleet records.
New cargo/bulk examples need a hull descriptor shared by grid/editor/runtime;
they cannot be made by giving the 14 m trawler a different label. Keep examples
as isolated drafts/showcases until commissioning has its own tested migration.

3. Equipment integration map (paths relative to repository)

Fishing: scripts/ship/fishing_system.gd owns trawling, catch timing, drag, ropes
and the currently primitive rotating trommel. Replace _setup_visuals with a
Blender fixed frame + separate drum; preserve anchored_to_brick and catch-hold
checks. This system requires CatchHoldComponent, not decorative crates.

Fish unloading: scripts/port/fish_landing_pump.gd owns connection, pumping,
flushing and completion. _ship_connection_world uses the first catch hold's
get_hose_drop_world. Replace static _build_visual hardware; keep the dynamically
curved hose and actual CatchLot/ShoreRswTankBank transfer. Related layout/job:
scripts/port/fish_landing_layout.gd and fish_landing_equipment_job.gd. The current
RSW pump presentation should not imply every kind of trawler lands fish by pump.

Bulk: scripts/ship/bulk_hold_component.gd has configurable width/length/depth
and a separate BulkHoldState. Give a new hold actual open geometry and keep fill
presentation derived from that state. Existing defaults are 6 x 12 x 2.5 m;
these are game defaults, not proof they fit a proposed hull. Coaming, cover panel
and moving hinges/lifting points should be independent assets.

Cranes: scripts/port/bulk_crane.gd uses named ModelAssembler parts and shell
jaws; _apply_slew, _apply_boom, _apply_hoist and _apply_bucket drive transforms.
Keep the bucket detached from cable scaling, and retain grab_from_hold,
force_release_at and lot transfer. Its operator still has old pose helpers:
use the current character rig when touching this area. provision_crane.gd has
slew/trolley/hoist and ContainerNode attachment. Preserve cargo/job APIs and
working envelopes when adapting Blender parts; do not replace either controller.

Mooring: scripts/port/mooring_component.gd (NOT scripts/ship) uses grouped
ship_mooring_cleat and dock_mooring_bollard nodes, dynamic sag and tension solve.
Static assets belong on MooringPoint/MooringPost with the same rope anchor.
Blender cleats, bollards and fairleads must not move the semantic endpoint
without a deliberate adapter. Keep ropes dynamic and authoritative tied state.

Lighting: scripts/ship/ship_lighting.gd gathers ShipLight descendants and owns
OFF/NAV/WORK/ALL plus weather/daylight scaling. ShipLight has build_housing=false
for imported fixtures. Current NAV_MASTHEAD is explicitly all-round: this is
not a correct substitute for the forward masthead sector. Add proper sector
visibility and separate operating signals before claiming navigation lighting
finished. Keep deck floods independent from navigation signals.

4. Primary reference notebook and modeling implications

Damen portfolio, printed pages 31 and 35 (PDF indexes 30 and 34), visually
inspected as rendered pages:
https://medialibrary.damen.com/m/716b9459b2fa3e81/original/Portfolio-booklet.pdf
The cargo silhouette has aft accommodation and a long unobstructed central
cargo region. Fishing silhouettes differ in gantry, mast and boom arrangement.
Inference for our kit: reusable wheelhouse/roof modules, distinct working deck
arrangements and separate equipment; don't decorate three identical hulls.

Damen CF3850 current product page:
https://www.damen.com/vessels/cargo/multi-purpose-cargo-vessels/combi-freighter-3850
Reference: 89.75 x 12.50 m with 62.5 x 10.1 m hold; fixed-pitch propeller listed.
Use the relationship between cargo space, side passages and accommodation,
not these full-size dimensions as an automatic game hull specification.

MacGregor hatch families:
https://www.macgregor.com/products/merchant/hatch-covers/
https://www.macgregor.com/products/merchant/hatch-covers/lift-away-hatch-covers/
Lift-away panels and folding/rolling alternatives support distinct reusable
cover families. First implementation choice: separate coaming + lift-away panels
with lifting sockets; reserve a real stowage footprint. A closed lid alone is
not a functional hold. Operating covers need authority/state work separately.

MacGregor Triplex equipment brochure (text inspected, PDF image unavailable
through direct download: HTTP 403):
https://www.macgregor.com/globalassets/picturepark/imported-assets/83834.pdf
Pages 6-10 distinguish net handling machinery from telescopic deck cranes.
Three-roller purse-seine handling is not automatically a trawl drum. Research
the chosen fishing method further before selecting a working gear set. Model
pedestal, slew body, boom, cylinder, sheave and hook independently.

Wartsila rudder reference:
https://www.wartsila.com/encyclopedia/term/rudder
Conventional rudders pivot vertically behind the propeller. Supports the shaft-Z,
stock-Y arrangement, not the old models' placement inside our closed transom.

USCG published International COLREG text (use International columns):
https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/navigation-rules-amalgamated
Rules 21/23/26 and Annex I are the lighting reference. Distinguish masthead
225 degrees, each sidelight 112.5 degrees and sternlight 135 degrees. Active
trawling uses green over white; making way adds sidelights/sternlight. Keep
fishing state separate from ordinary underway state and from work lights.
Recheck placement/height requirements for each finished vessel length.
