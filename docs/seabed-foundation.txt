Seabed foundation - 11 October 2026

WorldLayout now provides seeded continuous shelves, banks and basins below the
existing coastline. Heights share the terrain datum; sample_water_depth accepts
an explicit water level, and sample_bottom_type provides initial rock/sand/mud
classification. SEABED_VERSION is separate from coastline identity so this pass
does not reroll captain seeds or move existing harbour sites. Chart caches include
that version. Future world/server compatibility must include seabed version when
bottom depth becomes authoritative for grounding or navigation.

WorldTerrainStreamer retains the clipped coastal shelf and fills its water-side
complement. Bottom meshes use existing terrain LODs, background construction and
sourced rock/soil materials. Fully submerged chunks do not cast shadows. Existing
land-only collision policy is retained: this pass adds no beaching/damage rules.
Depth values describe the natural seabed; port terrain overrides must be sampled
through sample_render_terrain_height for future local equipment/grounding.

The chart includes depth bands and a hover depth/bottom readout. The fishing
heatmap renderer, toggle and shortcut are removed. Old fishing preferences cannot
restore it. Trawl catch rules remain unchanged pending physical fish populations.

Seabed is the immediate milestone. Next fishing direction: variants of the
existing trawler for crab pots and rod/multi-hook fishing (including cod), natural
fish discovery, and potentially sonar. No school, sonar, pot, navigation-mark or
new boat implementation is included in this foundation.

Validation: three-seed seabed/depth/mesh-boundary and chart/weather cache checks;
terrain mesh/streamer and background tests; coastal continuity test (210 preserved
port placements); port placer test. Rendered cutaway and production depth chart
inspected, with captures archived in Pictures/machinescreenshots.
