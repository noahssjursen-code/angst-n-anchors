CUSTOM PORT HOME / WALK CLEARANCE - 15 OCTOBER 2026

Reproduced defect
  Home previously returned the first foundation's centroid without considering
  construction above it. A legal wall crossing the centre of an 8m foundation
  put the player at (4, 0.15, 4), inside the wall collider. Native H reproduced
  this and the capsule still intersected the wall immediately after Walk.

Correction
  CustomPortHomePlacement is a pure-data candidate search. It checks a padded
  footprint enclosing the actual walking capsule against the exact union of
  foundation polygons, including triangle/chamfer cutouts and deck holes.
  It searches near each tile's centre before moving to another tile.

  Authored collider bounds below the player's head are obstacles. Roofs at 3m
  do not block an ordinary player. Composite machinery uses its occupied areas,
  including the separate pump and tank rectangles, rather than the complete
  20 x 16m plant envelope. Saved doorways are conservatively avoided by Home
  because their moving colliders are local to an authored hinge frame.

  Once the scene exists, candidate positions also query the actual player's
  capsule against current live colliders. The existing-player safety check uses
  these real colliders, not conservative data envelopes: an open door and a
  clear fish-pump aisle remain valid standing positions. Walking after building
  equipment around the player now relocates them when their capsule is blocked.

  The original dry-land fallback is retained, with live clearance checking.
  It cannot use old unexcavated terrain heights underneath a constructed deck.
  No safe deck/land candidate means no teleport. Ship-mounted players retain
  their existing vessel path. No home coordinate or physical pose is persisted.
