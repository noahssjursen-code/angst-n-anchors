Compact world trial — 10 October 2026

Trying it
Single player -> New captain -> Company setup -> Sailing area -> Compact trial.
This creates a new 30 x 30 km world (75% of standard width/length; 56.25% of
standard area). Standard remains 40 x 40 km. Existing captains retain their own
world dimensions, seed, coordinates and geography. No save migration is needed.

The existing deterministic WorldConfig/WorldLayoutGenerator size pipeline drives
the actual coast SDF, islands, fjords, terrain heights, forests, port siting,
navigation and chart. This is not a scaled scene or closer port icons. Terrain
uses a 193 x 193 field, retaining the standard 156.25 m field sample spacing.
Ships, buildings, local terrain detail, elevations, bollard spacing, port
footprints and the 650 m approach-clearance checks stay in physical metres.
Port sites regenerate to fit the smaller geography; same-numbered ports are not
one-to-one scaled versions of the old world. Ocean and locomotion are unchanged.

These use actual MarineRoutePlanner passage distances. 'Nearest' means the
geographically nearest other port, not necessarily a compatible cargo destination.
Each home sample is all 34 other ports. Smaller area does not guarantee a 25%
reduction on each trip: home location, available port sites and channel detours
change. Short local jobs are promising; lengthy cross-world jobs remain lengthy.
Cargo offer destinations/rewards were not retuned in this experiment.

Verification
- Three seeds at both sizes: 840 checks, zero failures; 35 ports per map,
  full-sized land footprints/approach water, and valid nearest/home routes.
- Actual new-captain company selector -> compact home-port chart -> confirmation
  -> live world -> scratch save/reload. Preview/live terrain checksum agrees.
  The forest field uses the new bounds and all 35 ports register. No captain
  outside the isolated review directories was read or modified by the fixture.
- Loading an older captain without dimensions after a compact preview explicitly
  restores standard size; it no longer inherits the preview's session setting.
- WorldGenerationContext rejects explicitly different dimensions even while a
  checksum is unavailable. Missing legacy context fields remain compatible.
- Existing world_config_size_test and world_context_test pass. Nine pre-existing
  v8 layout/field hashes (three seeds at 10/40/120 km) are byte-identical to their
  recorded baselines. Generation version and existing geometry are unchanged.
- Daytime Godot harbour/channel captures inspected after terrain streaming drains.
  Compact review: about 10 ms median wall frame, roughly 1.4-1.9 ms GPU in the
  reviewed 1600x900 views on RTX5070. Different harbour geometry means this is a
  local cost check, not a controlled performance claim across all gameplay.
- High aerial review views expose pre-existing horizon/ocean coverage boundaries;
  these are not approved aerial presentation. Normal harbour/channel views are
  the useful scale references. Do not alter the accepted ocean as part of this trial.
