Bridge landfalls and coverage (2026-10-12)

BridgeStructurePlan now receives the same port grading zones as terrain and
uses the 25 m ground surface used by roadside scenery. Bank height, approach
landings, tower/pier bottoms and abutments all use this ground. Approach search
tries seven inland headings, with an 8.5% grade limit and terrain obstruction
checks. Unmapped crossings have a 22 m minimum deck; mapped waterways retain
the existing 55 m protected opening.

Bridge selection permits 56 candidates instead of 32, including up to 12
mainland shortcuts. Minimum shore gap is 60 m and shortcut spacing is 450 m.
A rejected crossing gets up to four nearby alternatives for the same land pair.
Suspension tower positions can move along the span while retaining foundation
depth, shipping opening and maximum main-span constraints.

BridgeRoadConnector adds terrain-following ties to nearby settlement and port
roads. It tries direct links, then a bounded local 25 m path search where needed.
Every route is checked at 2 m intervals with nine cross-width surface samples for dry ground and grade; its end meets
the existing road elevation. Surface and collision are included in the bridge
batches. Vegetation clearances follow the separate bridge and connector paths.
Planning remains on the existing background worker.

Validation: four structural families and three world seeds passed. Graded
landings fixture verifies an original 90 m hillside cut to 2 m, matching road
ends and buried foundations. Inlet fixture verifies a dry detour. World fixture
seed 1850239324 built 47 bridges (previous build: 15), rejecting 8 unsuitable
crossings; 8 landfalls found valid existing-road ties. Captures are archived
under Pictures/machinescreenshots/bridge-landfall-*.

Limit: the surrounding road network is still sparse. This does not create a
complete regional road network or guarantee an existing-road tie on every bank.
Invalid water/grade links are left unbuilt, not drawn through unsuitable terrain.

Placement and streaming follow-up (2026-10-12)
Large uninhabited islands no longer count as destinations. They may still serve
as intermediate links between inhabited land masses. Mainland shortcuts are
limited to two per world, water gaps up to 350 m, distinct destinations within
1200 m of each end, and no short land detour (at least a 5 km search threshold).
Accepted spans AND approach corridors must stay 40 m apart; alternatives are
checked as well. Selection order stays deterministic, independent of camera.
Terrain samples are cached per design. Completed plans publish progressively.
Assembly prioritizes distance to the whole bridge corridor. Bridges render
within 12 km, hide beyond 13.5 km, and retain their built resources for immediate
return; collision is enabled within 1.5 km. This is distance streaming, not a
full simplified-mesh LOD system. Main-thread assembly can still cause a hitch.
