Workshop walking after distant freecam

The construction camera can travel beyond the workshop terrain's 12 km visual
radius. That unloads the paused player's terrain. Previously, Walk waited for
the player's collider while leaving the remote camera as the only request
focus, so the missing ground was never requested.

The workshop now temporarily uses WorldTerrainStreamer's existing loading
priority at the walking destination. Player physics stays paused until the
current earthworks and nearby collider are ready. Switching to the player
camera releases that priority. Build, map, inspector, document picker,
document replacement and scene exit also release the workshop's request.
H uses the same destination request when returning home while walking.
The general world terrain streaming rules were not changed.

Focused regression: tests/custom_port_walk_streaming_test.tscn
Run with --headless -- --shipyard-playtest --empty-custom-port.
The actual generated-world scene covers natural terrain eviction after 14.5 km
of freecam travel, Walk recovery, cancellation by Build/map/New harbour, and H
returning home with its collider absent. It does not replace native visual or
foundation/terrain geometry tests.

Recorded 2026-10-15: baseline stayed blocked with no nearby terrain requests
for all 242 observations over two seconds. After the fix, 46 checks passed;
Walk recovered in 117 ms, cancellation recovery in 91 ms, and H in 118 ms on
this machine. These timings are observations, not a loading-time guarantee.
