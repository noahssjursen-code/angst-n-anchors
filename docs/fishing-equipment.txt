Crab pots — 12 October 2026

The Coastal Crabber's twelve creels are the traps. Press F while looking at a
creel. The hands bind to its two handles, the creel comes up, and it is thrown
over the side. Letting go of F does nothing, because the action is not a hold.
The trap then sinks on its own. Once it is on the bottom it belongs to the
company, not to the hull that threw it. Recalling the crabber leaves the buoy
where it was set. Bringing that crabber, or another crabber of the same company,
back alongside hauls it. The line boat cannot. It soaks until the next in-game
day (24 game hours, 24 real minutes). Each buoy shows the hours left. Hauling is
a second press at the pot hauler, only when that buoy is alongside and the soak
is finished. The hauler runs until the creel is up; the key does not have to
stay down. Pots set in about 10–35 m. Catch, bait and saving a deployed pot
through quit/reload are not part of this interaction. The set-pot record is the
piece a later server would replicate.

Fishing boat equipment — 12 October 2026

The flat harpe follows the supplied hand-winder photo: moulded handle opening,
white wound line, green keeper, six small hooks and a red teardrop sinker.
Its stored tackle is hidden while deployed. The heavy sea rod has a shorter,
thicker blank and a transverse reel. Its separate chrome pilk has split rings,
a tapered body and a red-sleeved treble hook, following the supplied pilk photo.
Blender sources: build_harpe.py, build_heavy_pilk.py, build_fishing_items.py.

Both use RMB to drop/lower directly beneath the equipment tip, LMB to retrieve.
The line can be set from the surface down to 180 m. Water deeper than that still
fishes: a 50 m set is allowed over a 254 m seabed, and the line stops at 180 m.
No charged throw is used by the station. A vertical obstruction check rejects
lowering through the hull/deck: stand at the rail with the tip over water.
Initial balance: harpe six hooks, maximum 5 kg each; heavy pilk one fish,
minimum 1.5 kg. Pilk lowering 6 m/s; harpe 3.5 m/s. These are gameplay tuning
values, not claims about real fishing gear limits. Actual school specimens are
filtered before selecting a bite, preserving shared sonar/school positions.
The fight continues hauling with either control; stay in the white bar.

Harbourmaster Equipment shop purchases and equips harpe for the active vessel.
The ship stores the selection; its portable mount swaps rod/harpe. Equipping
while held is rejected. Each harpe fish becomes its own catch-crate lot.
