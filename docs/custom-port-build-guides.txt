Construction reach and berth guides

Selecting or moving a crane shows its horizontal operating radius around the
actual slew pivot, including the inner dead zone. The fish landing plant shows
its hose reach around the real connection point. Package rotation and the
port's world origin are applied once. These are temporary construction guides;
the cargo yard stays plain asphalt.

The berth outlines use the same mooring polygons derived by the authority from
the quay and bollards. They show the maximum mooring envelope, not a promised
fit for an individual vessel. Cargo operations still check the full ship hull,
handling type, yard pairing, stock, reservations and access permissions.

G hides these guides with the construction grid. Walking, map/cursor mode and
ordinary non-equipment placement hide the active reach ring. Unchanged previews
reuse the overlay mesh. Nothing is added to the saved port document.

Native tests/custom_port_build_guides_test.tscn passed 18 checks for the fish
plant and 18 for the complete cargo harbour (--cargo-guides). These use the
actual workshop, compare the guide pivot to its authority-issued service,
exercise rotation and UI toggles, and verify the document is unchanged.
