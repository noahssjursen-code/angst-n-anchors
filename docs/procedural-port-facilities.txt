Procedural port facilities — 6 October 2026
==========================================

Implemented in port generation 48. PortLandPlan embeds a version-2 facility_plan
in the initial graph. PortFacilityPlan groups active import/export commodities
into shared functional precincts, links each to realized compatible berth IDs,
then packs metre-sized parcels inside the traced paved foundation. A waterfront
collector, rear collector and inland route are reserved before packing; facilities receive clear
access connections. Unplaceable facilities are explicitly recorded in unmet.
The planner does not silently enlarge terrain or overlap another facility.

Office/parking, general cargo shelters, fishing shelters/drains, empty container
storage slots, open bulk bays, grain silos, diesel tanks, crude tanks and insulated
gas vessels are supported. Liquid product identities remain separate. General
imports and exports share a yard. Existing berth cranes, unloading equipment,
trade identities, NPC commands and cargo authority remain in the existing systems.
Player/staff spawn positions use the public office forecourt when one fits.

Fourteen individual Blender assets accompany their source .blend files and generators:
harbour_office, cargo_shelter_8m, kerb_2m, wheel_stop, bulk_divider_4m,
yard_fence_4m, empty_pallet, grain_silo, liquid_tank, insulated_gas_vessel,
harbour_authority_20m, lng_terminal_tank_24m, yard_pallet, loaded_storage_rack.
PortFacilityVisual composes these; it does not bake an entire port into one mesh.
Fences use MultiMesh. Simple deliberate walking collision covers structures,
fences, parking stops and dividers. Quay lamps switch on during the night through
the existing WeatherLighting presentation state; there is no new time authority.

Saved graphs preserve the exact facility records. Graphs without facility_plan
retain their old apron/blueprint rendering path; no established port is regenerated.
Changing the initial generation recipe requires the usual generation version bump.

Visual reference: Port of Blyth Terminal Plans, inspected pages 3 and 4 for
office/welfare, parking, port roads, open storage and specialist terminal layout:
https://portofblyth.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/POB-Terminal-Plans-Brochure.pdf
This is an original compact game interpretation, not a reconstruction of Blyth.

Remaining limits
----------------
These are initial layout and presentation facilities. New shore-side storage
inventory/capacity, leasing, production and vehicle pathfinding are not implemented.
Existing quay gameplay remains authoritative. Office is an exterior shell, not an
enterable building; fish shelters are not an implemented cold chain. Tank models
are illustrative and do not claim engineering specifications. Current materials
are separate named regions but still need the requested worn PBR texture pass.
Public-road continuation beyond the foundation,
yard gates and varied office architecture remain future refinements. Large empty
apron areas are retained rather than filled with fake cargo. This is not final art
acceptance for every generated coastline or a finished port economy.

Road correction after visual review
-----------------------------------
HarbourRoadSurface unions quay, connection and apron road polygons before drawing
their perimeter. Intersections have no internal edge stripes; centre dashes use
a fixed 6m repeat / 2.5m paint length and stop through crossing roads. Yard entrances
are open. The pavement shader is continuous in world metres, with no per-piece UV
restart. Road paint uses screen-filtered width and a small depth-tested view bias
to prevent coplanar flicker and subpixel dotted edges. This applies to parking and
yard marking strips too. The new real-harbour daylight captures 1791315810–14 show
both the wide view and office junction. Walking and collision checks still pass.

Scale and access revision (6 October)
------------------------------------
Noah withdrew the decorative container-yard request. No automatic container depot,
ambient containers, order records or additional container inventory are generated.
Existing freight-container facilities retain their empty real-size storage slots.

The new harbour authority is a 20 x 14m four-storey exterior with glazed control
floor, hipped roof, canopy, escape stairs and mast. Its office/parking plot is
48 x 48m (40 x 44m fallback), with a separate 2.4m footway and 4cm raised paving.
NPCs and player spawn outside its public entrance; the closed shell blocks entry.
LNG plots are 88 x 64m (64 x 56m fallback), with two 24m-diameter terminal tanks,
22m shells, roof rails, access ladder and transfer pipework. Tank collision follows
the round shell rather than an invisible square. Oil tanks and grain banks also
use industrial scale. General/fishing yards include instanced open pallet stacks
and loaded shelving; these props confer no freight inventory or capacity.
Bulk bays use 12m walls on larger plots, with access from the centre aisle.

Planning rotates into the coastline chord frame and stores each parcel's yaw.
Front or rear entrances connect to the nearest unobstructed collector. A second
packing attempt uses smaller supported auxiliary-yard footprints only when that
fits more facilities. Foundation area is retained; oversized facilities report
unmet instead of shrinking their models. Persisted/evolved graphs are untouched.

Road boundaries have 3m turning fillets, overlapping buffered centrelines and
continuous dash phase through gentle bends. Narrow offset return spikes are
removed before triangulation. Yard parcels no longer have white outline boxes.
Four-sample geometry antialiasing reduces fence and kerb shimmer. Road surface
shading uses world-metre coordinates, and flat overlays do not cast shadows.

Asphalt cargo staging and startup regression
-------------------------------------------
Quay CargoSlotPadComponent bounds remain registered with the harbour/crane but
show_pad_visual is false: no slab, grid or orange fill. The yard origin is the
asphalt crown and container placement has zero extra visual-pad offset. Existing
ship pads retain their visual/clearance behavior. The isolated harbour test stages
a real ContainerUnit, checks its floor height and captures container-on-asphalt.

World generation expands records for all 35 ports (needed by terrain and shipping
lanes), but models are proximity-loaded. Facility packing was scanning every
lattice position with expensive polygon tests. Sorting by a cheap score lower
bound and stopping when no remaining candidate can win reduced the same planner
fixtures from 6.9-7.5 seconds to 0.68 seconds. Overlap/support and deterministic
round-trip checks still pass. No models are built by the planner.

ProximityLoader is paused until the real player camera is ready, avoiding builds
around the temporary zero-position observer. The rendered world boot test at
seed 424242 / 40km reached boot_finished in 14.317 seconds, with one home-port
model and 34 unloaded registrations (previous optimized run instantiated six
ports during boot and took 15.531 seconds). This is a cold test on this machine,
not a claim of the user's previous seven-second join time. World-layout generation
alone measured 5.83 seconds. Full-world test teardown still reports renderer RID
leaks; no runtime script errors occurred before the boot checks passed.
