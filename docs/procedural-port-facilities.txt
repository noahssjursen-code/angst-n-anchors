Procedural port facilities — 6 October 2026
==========================================

Implemented in port generation 47. PortLandPlan embeds a version-1 facility_plan
in the initial graph. PortFacilityPlan groups active import/export commodities
into shared functional precincts, links each to realized compatible berth IDs,
then packs metre-sized parcels inside the traced paved foundation. A waterfront
collector and inland route are reserved before packing; facilities receive clear
access connections. Unplaceable facilities are explicitly recorded in unmet.
The planner does not silently enlarge terrain or overlap another facility.

Office/parking, general cargo shelters, fishing shelters/drains, empty container
storage slots, open bulk bays, grain silos, diesel tanks, crude tanks and insulated
gas vessels are supported. Liquid product identities remain separate. General
imports and exports share a yard. Existing berth cranes, unloading equipment,
trade identities, NPC commands and cargo authority remain in the existing systems.
Player/staff spawn positions use the public office forecourt when one fits.

Ten individual Blender assets accompany their source .blend files and generator:
harbour_office, cargo_shelter_8m, kerb_2m, wheel_stop, bulk_divider_4m,
yard_fence_4m, empty_pallet, grain_silo, liquid_tank, insulated_gas_vessel.
PortFacilityVisual composes these; it does not bake an entire port into one mesh.
Fences use MultiMesh. Simple deliberate walking collision covers structures,
fences, parking stops and dividers. Quay lamps switch on during the night through
the existing WeatherLighting presentation state; there is no new time authority.

Saved graphs preserve the exact facility records. Graphs without facility_plan
retain their old apron/blueprint rendering path; no established port is regenerated.
Changing the initial generation recipe requires the usual generation version bump.

Visual reference: Port of Blyth Terminal Plans, inspected pages 3 and 4 for
office/welfare, parking, port roads, open storage and specialist terminal layout:
https://portofblyth.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/POB-Terminal-Plans-Brochure.pdf
This is an original compact game interpretation, not a reconstruction of Blyth.

Remaining limits
----------------
These are initial layout and presentation facilities. New shore-side storage
inventory/capacity, leasing, production and vehicle pathfinding are not implemented.
Existing quay gameplay remains authoritative. Office is an exterior shell, not an
enterable building; fish shelters are not an implemented cold chain. Tank models
are illustrative and do not claim engineering specifications. Current materials
are separate named regions but still need the requested worn PBR texture pass.
Public-road continuation beyond the foundation,
yard gates and varied office architecture remain future refinements. Large empty
apron areas are retained rather than filled with fake cargo. This is not final art
acceptance for every generated coastline or a finished port economy.

Road correction after visual review
-----------------------------------
HarbourRoadSurface unions quay, connection and apron road polygons before drawing
their perimeter. Intersections have no internal edge stripes; centre dashes use
a fixed 6m repeat / 2.5m paint length and stop through crossing roads. Yard entrances
are open. The pavement shader is continuous in world metres, with no per-piece UV
restart. Road paint uses screen-filtered width and a small depth-tested view bias
to prevent coplanar flicker and subpixel dotted edges. This applies to parking and
yard marking strips too. The new real-harbour daylight captures 1791315810–14 show
both the wide view and office junction. Walking and collision checks still pass.
