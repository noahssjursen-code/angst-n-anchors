CUSTOM PORT INSPECTOR HOST LAYOUT

The inspector measures wrapping content only while visible. Godot does not
sort hidden containers; their old child widths can be a single pixel, yielding
an artificial 1656px minimum for a panel configured at 620px. Applying that
hidden minimum made Dock/Leave actions disappear below the actual workshop.

Show, tab changes and viewport resize now schedule a visible layout pass.
After the normal container passes, the configured panel size is restored and
its bounds are kept within the viewport with a 16px margin. Long body content
remains scrollable; the action rows remain outside that scroll. No fields or
actions are removed to fit the panel. Workshop anchors are unchanged.
