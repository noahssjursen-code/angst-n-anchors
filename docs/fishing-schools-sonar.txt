Fishing schools and blue sounder — 12 October 2026

The same FishingHabitat members drive individual meshes, sonar targets, bait attraction and catch depletion. Bait uses a 70 m horizontal radius around the school centre, a 6 m vertical tolerance around a live member, and local terrain samples to reject attraction through land/reefs. This is a gameplay attraction model, not an acoustic or fish-behaviour simulator. Proximity queries are bounded; no global population is instantiated.

The downward sounder has a 20-degree half-angle. Fish return range is sqrt(vertical_depth^2 + horizontal_offset^2), which produces arches from actual movement through the beam. The UI additionally shows current schools' mean vertical depths as Fish ... m for line payout. Historical echoes may remain after leaving a school. Right edge is newest. Large number is current seabed depth. No fictional voltage is displayed because vessels do not expose battery voltage.

SonarDisplay renders 90 half-second intervals, plus one predecessor ping for smooth interpolation. One cached 360 x 294 texture, only four new columns per ordinary ping, native image blit for retained history. Full repaint only for history/range/ship changes. Scroll is render-rate; habitat ping remains 2 Hz. MSDF font copy keeps large instrument text crisp without changing shared UI fonts.

F3 -> G -> Fish schools & catch areas: actual fish marks, coloured 70 m radius cylinders/depth bands and grouped school labels, visible through water. A cylinder is an attraction envelope; terrain can occlude parts. Disabled layer performs no habitat work. Active review is limited to 16 schools within 350 m and updates at 2 Hz.

Run scenes/showcases/sonar_showcase.tscn (F6; automatically isolated). Left/right cycle deep/empty/shallow grounds; Space pauses transmission. --capture verifies measured returns, 65 m offset bait access, incremental/full raster equivalence, smooth interpolation, pause and ship-change clearing. Actual gameplay integration also verified in shipyard_playtest on a cargo vessel: sonar defaults on, displays in helm HUD and measures generated seabed. At the helm, N cycles the sounder through full size, chart size and hidden. Transmission keeps running in every size. The display sits on the dashboard, immediately left of the minimap.
