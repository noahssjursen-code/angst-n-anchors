COASTAL SKERRIES / GENERATION 12
12 October 2026

Goal
Keep the broad fjord geography and add the smaller exposed islands and fragments
missing between mainland and open water. These are terrain, not decorative props.

Implemented
- A deterministic, spatially indexed coastal feature layer, independent of the
  78.125 m macro raster. Patchy groups have a parent islet, smaller fragments,
  bent/notched outlines, short submerged feet, and real intervening water gaps.
- 675 / 764 / 781 exposed features for seeds 42 / 1212599375 / 77127.
  527 / 556 / 566 of these belong to groups of at least three.
- One geographic source feeds visible terrain, depth, land checks, chart outlines,
  navigation occupancy, and physical collision. Ports remain on the macro coast.
- Existing main shipping corridors stay clear. Navigation detects sub-grid rocks,
  refines difficult short passages, and refuses invalid routes rather than
  stitching an unchecked straight line through land.
- Distant terrain retains exposed outlines. Nearby streamed chunks add more
  subdivisions; detailed meshes are built on workers. No full-resolution global
  terrain mesh or giant fine world raster is generated.
- Detail triangulation now preserves full chunk coverage and shared edge samples.
- Submerged formations superseded by a new island are removed from the active
  formation set, preventing compact hazard records becoming broad island shores.
- Close-up map tiles bake on one bounded background worker, with an eight-tile
  cache and an overview available while baking. Worker shutdown is explicitly joined.

Research basis
NGU seabed mapping and MAREANO imagery informed the distinction between fractured
bedrock groups, exposed highs, submerged companions, and intervening depressions.
These are procedural design parameters, not measurements calibrated to a raw
Karmoy bathymetric grid and not an exact recreation of the reference location.
https://static.ngu.no/upload/Publikasjoner/Rapporter/2023/2023_008.pdf
https://www.mareano.no/resources/images/2015/chapter-3.pdf
https://static.ngu.no/Mareano/Landskap.html

Godot's documented visibility/LOD approach informed retaining silhouettes while
reducing distant surface complexity and keeping expensive detail local.
https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/tutorials/3d/visibility_ranges.html
https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.6/classes/class_arraymesh.html

Review / remaining limits
The critic found no remaining geographic blocker. Some tops still look too grassy
and smooth, and some medium silhouettes share an elongated family. That material
and small surface-relief work remains an art refinement. Do not claim scan accuracy.
Routing still uses the existing fixed shore clearance, not vessel-specific draft.
Geometry is rebuilt on demand; this pass does not add persistent mesh files on disk.
