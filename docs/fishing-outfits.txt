COASTAL FISHING — research and implementation, 12 October 2026

CURRENT REVISION — supersedes the historical controls and lineup below
Rod fishing takes place on deck. F picks up/returns the carried rod. Hold LMB
then release to cast; once cast, HOLD RMB lets line out/sinks the tackle, HOLD
LMB reels in, and releasing both holds depth. Both buttons together hold too.
No automatic retrieval. LMB strikes a bite. Sonar remains on every boat's helm
HUD, enabled by default. The builder playtest uses the bounded coast sandbox.

The smaller fishing HUD receives render-rate updates through LocalPlayerView;
the general voyage snapshot remains at 10 Hz. Actual D3D12 live tests measured
about 60 distinct depth updates/second. Habitat probes remain bounded at 4 Hz.
Six original Blender fish now have skin textures, differentiated anatomy, swim
rigs, and MouthHook sockets. A hooked fish faces the rod and the line ends at
its lip at any specimen size. The deck crate shows individual rotatable 3D fish,
length, weight, rarity, size grade and NOK value. Whole fish cannot be split,
merged or duplicated; save/load and slow whole-fish shore transfer are tested.
NOK/kg tuning: cod 80, pollack 110, saithe 55, mackerel 60, haddock 120, halibut 250.
Internal legacy marks fields remain only for data compatibility.

Additional references: IMR species pages (https://www.hi.no/en/hi/temasider/species),
PENN Pursuit IV spinning-combo proportions, and the official Fisch description
(https://www.roblox.com/games/16732694052/Fisch). No claim of playing Fisch or
copying its assets. Fish art is original and awaits user visual approval.
The small trawler is retired. Crabber is a distinct builder preset; trap and
multi-hook gameplay, player inventory and multiplayer fishing are later work.
Nearby schools now render the same deterministic individuals queried by hooks
and sonar: six species, stable specimen sizes, 6 m hook attraction radius, and
actual fish inside the 20-degree sonar cone. Hooked fish leave their school;
landing removes that individual and escaping releases it. Depletion lasts for
this world session; persistent population depletion remains future work.
Rendering is capped at 192 fish within 180 m of the camera, with shared meshes,
4 Hz position queries and render-frame interpolation. School fish currently use
rigid swimming silhouettes; articulated swimming is used for the hooked fish.

Rod visuals run after player/camera positioning each rendered frame. A 25-point
constrained visual rope replaces the straight ray; it is not a full rope/collision
simulation. Rod flex responds to tension. Third-person equipment is anchored to
the player rather than the orbit camera. LMB after landing begins another cast.
Blender hand/digit bones now explicitly define +Y fingers and +Z knuckles;
fishing grips use that authored frame rather than guessing wrist orientation.
Rebuilt mariner.blend/GLB and verified neutral palms, morphs and animation lengths.

12 October follow-up validation: fishing_school_test (three consecutive catches,

HISTORICAL INITIAL STUDY FOLLOWS

Requested progression: hook / line / sinker, then crab traps, then trawler.
All three reuse the existing 14 x 5 m imported hull and wheelhouse. This is
an original game arrangement inspired by Norwegian sjarks, not a dimensional
replica of a MALO or a claim that any arbitrary hull can safely carry any gear.

REFERENCES
Jemar Norpower: https://www.jemar.no/malo-sjark
MALO 36: 10.99 m x about 4.45 m, working deck, configurable fishing equipment.
The smaller 23 ft pot boat also illustrates an open working deck and exterior
steering position. These support the layout choice, not our hull's ratings.
Photograph: https://www.jemar.no/uploads/oHC9CbQx/IMG_6969.jpg

Hydema: https://www.hydema.no/products/fish-farming-products/pot-haulers/
Column-mounted side hauler reference. Game module is an original simplified
pedestal, davit and powered sheave; no manufacturer logos or copied geometry.

FAO handlining: https://www.fao.org/4/t0511e/T0511E01.htm
Hook, line, sinker and bait; finding fish and setting tackle depth matter.
This supports a manual starter loop rather than granting automatic catches.

Belitronic: https://w97151.shop.textalk.se/en/bj5000-ex/jigging-machine-bj5000ex.html
Commercial electric jigging is a distinct machine, with line routing through
pulleys. It is not the manual rod fitted in this first implementation.

Mustad: https://mustadautoline.com/coastal-hydrelic-systems/
Coastal longlining needs hauling, baiting and hook/line storage equipment.
Future multi-hook upgrades must represent that handling rather than treating
a longline as a visually unchanged rod with a bigger catch multiplier.

Seafish methods: https://training.seafish.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/basic-fishing-methods.pdf
Pot fleets depend on working deck, seabed and gear handling; trawling uses a
different set of hauling gear. Reference only, not copied artwork.

LIMITATIONS / NEXT
Rod operates from helm; no hand/rod animation or visible fish model yet.
Schools are bounded analytic populations, depletion is session-only; catches
are persistent through existing inventory. Multiplayer fishing commands are
not implemented; hide the rod instrument for remote sessions.
Crab trapping and longlining gameplay are pending. Crab outfit is a visual
equipment study; detailed hatch, lashings, hydraulics and working animation
still need development before it is a finished operational crab boat.
Existing trawl catches use habitat productivity, not the rod's school depletion.
Species prices and habitat curves are game tuning, not real market/stock data.
No global school objects or whole-map fish bake added; local queries only.
