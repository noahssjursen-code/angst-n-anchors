Legacy whole-building placeholders
=================================

The custom port build library no longer offers the original Warehouse (12 x 8 x
6 m) or Workshop (8 x 8 x 4 m) solid boxes. Use the authored Workshop category's
4 m walls, working door, windows, open bay and roofs to construct a usable room.

Both original IDs remain in CustomPortCatalog with build_library_visible:false.
The flag only affects library choices. Saved JSON still validates and presents
the same mesh, collision dimensions, pose and custom attributes. Existing pieces
may still be moved or removed. This is not an authority/schema migration.

The existing whole-building models are not safe visual replacements without a
separate footprint/collision change. Godot measured the full imported meshes:
  warehouse_12x18: 12.62 x 20.56 m footprint, 6.89 m tall (including canopy)
  harbour_office: 12.66 x 10.38 m footprint, 4.985 m tall
  cargo_shelter_8m: 8.40 x 8.394 m footprint, 5.569 m tall
The warehouse and office are deliberately closed exterior shells. They are not
substituted or distorted to fit the legacy boxes.

Focused verification: tests/custom_port_library_test.tscn with
--headless -- --shipyard-playtest. It covers hidden choices, retained modular
pieces, search/categories, equip signals and legacy JSON/mesh/collider recovery.
