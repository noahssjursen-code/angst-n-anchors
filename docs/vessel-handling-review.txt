VESSEL HANDLING — 7 October 2026

Problem reproduced in actual stock BoatBody/Jolt/component simulation:
ASTERN requested 0.55 throttle and then applied a 0.45 reverse multiplier,
leaving 24.75% of available ahead thrust. At harbour speeds quadratic water
resistance barely slowed the imported vessels after generic damping had been
removed to restore their cruising speeds.

Changes:
- ASTERN now requests full reverse; propulsion still applies reverse efficiency.
- Imported machinery/hull profiles use 0.65 reverse efficiency.
- Imported hulls add water-relative longitudinal resistance below 5 m/s. It is
  strongest below 2 m/s and fades smoothly to zero between 2 and 5 m/s.
  This is deliberate gameplay handling tuning, not a calibrated naval model.
- The added force applies regardless of engine setting, in either direction.
  STOP never sets velocity to zero and no generic all-axis damping is restored.
- Greater stern-first hull resistance offsets the stronger reverse power.
  Ahead cruising resistance and engine power remain unchanged.
- Parameters live in HullPhysicsProfile; old non-imported hull drag defaults
  remain unchanged. The shared full-astern helm stage also affects legacy boats.
