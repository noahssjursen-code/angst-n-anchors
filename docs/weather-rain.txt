Local precipitation presentation
===============================
RainField reads WeatherLighting's composed precipitation; it never invents a
separate weather state. WeatherSample.supported_precipitation caps rain against
cloud cover. Rain quads use the actual scene light and depth rather than glow.

Roof shelter (10 October 2026)
- One world-aligned GPUParticlesCollisionHeightField3D covers80x80m locally,
  with64m vertical range and512x512 resolution. A snapped4m origin updates at
  most8Hz, including moving ship roofs; it does not follow the camera every frame.
- Rendering layer20 marks rain particles for collision. Ordinary geometry can
  occlude them; unrelated particle effects are not affected by this rain field.
- Drops spawn6-10m above the camera instead of a box extending below eye height.
  At60Hz, contact hiding includes the optical half-streak so tails do not flash
  through thin roofs. The collider stays until residual drops expire, then turns
  off entirely in dry weather.
- This is a top-down shelter approximation. It does not simulate wind recirculation
  through open windows, horizontal wall impacts, drips or impact splashes. It is
  bounded local rendering work, with no per-drop scene nodes or CPU ray queries.

Wet ground (10 October 2026)
- SurfaceWetness is local presentation driven by WeatherLighting.rain_amount,
  not another weather authority or persistent hydrology simulation. Entering a
  rainy world starts damp. Subsequent rain wets ground over tens of seconds;
  after rain stops, drying takes several minutes of real play time. Updates at
  most8Hz and stops uniform work when stable. Streamed materials inherit the
  current amount; weak references do not retain discarded terrain/materials.
- Quay paving and road paint opt in through HarbourEnvironmentKit. Village
  asphalt/gravel opt in explicitly through SurfaceMaterialLibrary's rain_exposed
  argument. Cached exposed and sheltered instances are separate. Default ship,
  cabin, clothing and imported finishes remain dry; material names alone never
  establish exposure. Future sheltered exterior geometry needs explicit masking.
- Original source maps remain unchanged. Damp asphalt darkens and gains a
  broad sheen, gravel stays rough. Both terrain LODs share habitat-dependent
  darkening: bedrock/soil stronger, heath restrained. Existing tidal dampness
  remains. Snow and distant canopy never inherit glossy ground roughness.
- No puddle geometry, water depth, impact splashes or screen reflection pass is
  added. This is a low-cost damp-surface response, not pooled water simulation.
- tests/wet_ground_review.tscn compares sourced finishes and both terrain LODs,
  checks temporal response, late streaming and sheltered material isolation.
  tests/wet_ground_world_review.tscn compares actual harbour day/night and inland
  terrain under identical weather, with archived images and timing reports.

Material response reference (porosity, roughness, accumulation and drying):
https://seblagarde.wordpress.com/2013/04/14/water-drop-3b-physically-based-wet-surfaces/

Weather sound alignment (10 October 2026)
- Atmosphere beds use supported local rain and wind, with the existing smooth
  onset/tail. Cloudiness and fog no longer trigger drizzle or storm recordings.
  Dry wind can select dry squall without requiring rain or low visibility.
- Sequential layers ignore missing recording slots. There is currently no
  rain_heavy asset; maximum rain retains the strongest available moderate loop
  instead of crossfading into an empty slot. No new sound assets were invented.
- tests/rain_presentation_review.tscn -- --shipyard-playtest --audio-only checks
  actual player gains in eight weather states; the ordinary rendered run also
  keeps these checks alongside the seven sky/rain views. This verifies activation
  and continuity of the existing recordings, not a newly mastered sound library.
