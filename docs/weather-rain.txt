Local precipitation presentation
===============================
RainField reads WeatherLighting's composed precipitation; it never invents a
separate weather state. WeatherSample.supported_precipitation caps rain against
cloud cover. Rain quads use the actual scene light and depth rather than glow.

Roof shelter (10 October 2026)
- One world-aligned GPUParticlesCollisionHeightField3D covers80x80m locally,
  with64m vertical range and512x512 resolution. A snapped4m origin updates at
  most8Hz, including moving ship roofs; it does not follow the camera every frame.
- Rendering layer20 marks rain particles for collision. Ordinary geometry can
  occlude them; unrelated particle effects are not affected by this rain field.
- Drops spawn6-10m above the camera instead of a box extending below eye height.
  At60Hz, contact hiding includes the optical half-streak so tails do not flash
  through thin roofs. The collider stays until residual drops expire, then turns
  off entirely in dry weather.
- This is a top-down shelter approximation. It does not simulate wind recirculation
  through open windows, horizontal wall impacts, drips or impact splashes. It is
  bounded local rendering work, with no per-drop scene nodes or CPU ray queries.
