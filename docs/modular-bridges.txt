MODULAR NORWEGIAN BRIDGES — 12 October 2026

Authoring: tools/build_bridge_kit.py, executed in Blender 5.2.2.
Ten separate editable .blend files in resources/models/_source/bridges.
Ten separate GLBs and kit manifest in resources/models/bridges.
Units metres. Godot -Z along bridge; origin on starting deck datum.
SocketStart/SocketEnd are exported. Unit structural members are dimensioned
by the generator; the road module is 20 x 12 m with 9.4 m roadway, raised
footpaths, edge/centre markings and railings. No whole-bridge asset is stored.
Original authored geometry; runtime finishes reuse SurfaceMaterialLibrary:
concrete_cast, asphalt, brushed_steel, roof_enamel (markings). Existing CC0
source URLs/licenses remain in resources/textures/marine's manifest.

Families: girder (<220 m), arch (220–420 m), cantilever (420–820 m),
suspension (820 m+). Terrain/navigation checks override these initial bands.
References: Risoy deck arch, Raftsund concrete cantilever, Bomla/Stord
suspension. These are game proportions, not engineering certifications.
Girder supports <=65 m spacing; cantilever <=280 m; suspension main span
<=1310 m. Reject foundations deeper than 90 m; reject supports within 60 m
of mapped waterway intersections; reserve deck height sea+64 m over those
waterways so the concrete underside remains above 55 m. Arch sites crossing
a mapped waterway switch to suspension. Invalid concrete support sites try
suspension before rejecting. Larger impossible spans remain unbuilt.

Approaches search up to 1200 m outward on dry terrain, maximum grade 8.5%.
Piers follow sampled ground and finish below the deck. Approach corridors
clear forest without flattening terrain. They do not yet join settlement
road graphs. No NPC road traffic, tunnels, chart bridge symbols or vessel
air-draft route planning was added in this pass. Shipping opening checks
use the geographic waterway centreline graph, not every possible ship route.

Runtime: CoastalBridges plans records on a worker, then assembles one bridge
per frame. BridgeAssembler stamps imported Blender parts and combines them
into four material batches per bridge. Shared metre-space textures avoid
stretching the finishes with unit members. Visual range 12 km. Static deck,
rail, pier, arch-rib and anchor collision is included; cables are visual.
No captain save modification or independent economy/world singleton.
F3 G bridge layer retains candidate/rejection diagnostics; finished bridges
are visible independently of debug settings.
