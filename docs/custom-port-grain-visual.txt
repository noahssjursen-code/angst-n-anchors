Grain bulk presentation
=======================

OreMoundBuilder now preserves grain as a third explicit bulk commodity. The
existing heap geometry is shared with coal and iron ore; its new cereal finish
is authored procedurally in resources/shaders/ore_pile.gdshader. No downloaded
textures or new model dependency are involved. Grain has warm dry colour,
high roughness, no metallic flakes, small settled variation and millimetre-scale
surface relief. Derivative filtering averages detail at distance.

The same material is used by custom-port stock bays, installed BulkHoldComponent
fill, the animated grab fill, temporary transfer mounds and falling cargo pieces.
The existing textured coal_aggregate/crushed_aggregate profiles and their
procedural fallback variants remain unchanged. Grain works with either material
library setting. No quantities, prices, hold capacity or physical rules changed.

Reproducible review:
  tests/custom_port_grain_visual_test.tscn -- --shipyard-playtest
Add --capture in a rendered run for archived native views of all three stock
bays, a close grain surface, and actual grain loading/unloading with the existing
bulk crane and Coastal Bulk hold. Screenshots go to Pictures/machinescreenshots.

The isolated fixture starts with finite 80-tonne stocks of each commodity,
loads 20 tonnes of grain, and unloads it again. It verifies material identity
through permanent and temporary visuals, the exact unchanged authority snapshot
during animation, restored stock balance, unaffected coal/ore, and cleanup.
Reviewed 15 October 2026: 28 headless checks and 34 native capture checks passed
without logged errors. This is presentation validation, not a new economy or
live multiplayer integration. Existing heap outlines and transfer particle
geometry are reused; they are not individual simulated cereal kernels.
