# Mooring boarding gangway

Original in-house Blender parts, generated by
`resources/models/_source/gangway/build_gangway.py`. Editable `.blend` sources
live beside that script. No downloaded model geometry.

- `boarding_gangway_4m.glb`: 0.90 m clear deck, structural side channels,
  crossmembers, grip strips, toe boards, stanchions and two-height guard rails.
  Authored length 4 m; origin/socket `ShipEnd` at the deck hinge and `ShoreEnd`
  4 m along local -Z in Godot. Main span adjusts to the actual gap.
- `gangway_landing_rollers.glb`: separate unscaled axle, rubber rollers and
  landing lip at the quay end.
- Named finishes reuse SurfaceMaterialLibrary's galvanized metal, nonslip deck,
  rubber and yellow working-steel profiles. Materials remain shared/cached;
  visual mesh details are excluded from the player's walking collision.

`ShipGangway` belongs to every ImportedDraftVessel. It derives deployment from
the existing MooringComponent and HarbourController berth assignment, without
writing a new save or authority record. It selects the quay-facing straight
1 m bulwark module with clear standing space behind it, hinges that actual
module and its collision outwards, and spans to a real clear quay surface.
This preserves the shipyard draft, paints and saved part placements.

The main walking body follows the ship-end hinge each physics frame. Its shore
endpoint is checked at 5 Hz. Floor and guard collisions are three simple boxes.
The span is unavailable if the quay is absent/blocked, span exceeds 8 m, slope
exceeds 31 degrees, or vessel speed exceeds 0.8 m/s. Casting off hides the span,
disables its collision and closes the gate. Fit-out hydration refreshes the
candidate gates and their moving colliders safely.

Current scope: all four imported starter vessels, both sides; custom imported
ships need a straight 1 m deck-level bulwark module and a clear entrance. No
legacy-hull adapter, manual rigging animation or visible stowed-ramp storage.
Deployment is derived locally from replicated mooring; a real two-client
boarding session has not been verified.

Run `tests/ship_gangway_test.tscn -- --shipyard-playtest` with the renderer enabled
(captured player input is unavailable in headless mode). `--rotated` also rotates
the quay and ship through +/-33 degrees. It uses the actual player controller
to walk both directions across all four ships, including roll/heave; verifies
cast-off, closed gates, invalid slope/reach, missing quay, save immutability and
fit-out refresh. Screenshots go to `C:/Users/noahs/Pictures/machinescreenshots`.
